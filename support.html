🎧 Support Information

This is an independent educational resource. It does not provide official Microsoft support, account access, or verified telephone numbers.

Stay safe

Use trusted Microsoft product experiences for sensitive account actions. Never share passwords, one-time verification codes, recovery codes, or full payment-card details. Be cautious of unsolicited calls, pop-ups, and messages asking for remote access or urgent payment.

Prepare for assistance

Record the product, device, exact error message, approximate time, and steps already attempted. For work or school accounts, contact your organization's IT administrator about settings controlled by the organization.
